Step 1

You leave before the light.

Hit Start run and go. Distance, time, pace and every hex you cross tick up live. Lock the screen, slide the phone into a pocket — pause or stop from the lock screen whenever you need to.

Step 2

Every turn is a bet.

The map doesn’t care how fast you are, only that you come home. End within 40 m of where you began and the banner turns: Loop closed — stop to claim territory!

≥ 300 m² enclosed · Not closed = no claim, nothing lost

Step 3

1 claim / day · Night runs start at level 3

Chapter IIINext morning — The Arsenal

Dig in. Strike back.

The first claim is luck. Keeping the land is craft. Five tactics, all in the app, none of them subtle.

  1. 01

    Fortify

    Spend coins and a hex holds longer. Raise its ceiling one level at a time, up to 10 — each step costs double the last, from 15 coins to 1,920. Run over your own land and it heals back to that ceiling.

    15L3 30L4 60L5 120L6 240L7 480L8 960L9 1,920L10
  2. 02

    Bounty

    Put a price on their land. Coins go into escrow, split across the hexes you pick, and the whole pile is paid to whoever lands the finishing hit. Change your mind and an unclaimed bounty is refunded.

  3. 03

    Ghost run

    Once a day, take ground without a warning. The victim sees the map change — they just never get the buzz.

    Ghost run — victims won't be notified
  4. 04

    Night run

    After nine at night the streets go quiet. Hexes you take from neutral ground between 21:00 and 06:00 start at defense level 3 instead of 2.

  5. 05

    Clans & weekly boards

    Run under a clan’s flag and the land you take belongs to all of you — anyone can heal or fortify it. Boards rank this week’s net area, claimed minus what was taken, and reset every seven days, so a latecomer can still end the week on top.

Chapter IVDay 30 — The Companion

It starts as a speck.

Every run feeds a hero. Coins from your kilometres unlock pixel-art companions and evolve them through fifteen forms. You begin with one, picked at random — and by the time it’s fully grown, you’ll know every street it carried you through.

A run under 5 km pays 1 coin and every further 5 km adds another; a daily login bonus builds across a 7-day streak.
