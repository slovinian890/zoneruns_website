Step 1

Start a run, any run.

Hit Start run and go. Distance, time, pace and the hexes you cross update live, and keep going with the screen locked. Pause or stop from the lock screen without unlocking.

Updates every 1.5 s · Lock-screen controls

Step 2

Come back to where you began.

The banner tells you how you're doing. End within 40 m of your start and it flips to Loop closed — stop to claim territory!

≥ 300 m² enclosed · Not closed = no claim, nothing lost

Step 3

Everything the loop touches is yours.

Stop and save. Every hex inside the loop is captured at defense level 2, and the run pays out coins. One claim per day, so pick the route like it matters.

1 claim / day · Night runs start at level 3

03 Rules of engagement

Five ways to make it personal.

  1. 01

    Fortify

    Spend coins to raise a hex's defense ceiling, one level at a time, up to 10. Each step costs double the last: 15 coins for level 3, 1,920 for level 10. Running over your own hex heals it back to that ceiling.

    15L3 30L4 60L5 120L6 240L7 480L8 960L9 1,920L10
  2. 02

    Bounty

    Put coins on enemy hexes. They're held in escrow, split across the hexes you pick, and paid out in full to whoever lands the finishing hit. Change your mind and an unclaimed bounty is refunded.

  3. 03

    Ghost run

    Once a day, take land without the victim getting an alert. They'll still see the map change. They just won't get the push.

    Ghost run — victims won't be notified
  4. 04

    Night run

    Hexes you take from neutral ground between 21:00 and 06:00 start at defense level 3 instead of 2. The streets are quieter, and so is the competition.

  5. 05

    Clans & weekly boards

    Run under a clan's flag and the hexes you take belong to it. Any member can heal or fortify them. Boards rank net area this week, claimed minus what was taken from you, and reset every 7 days so latecomers aren't locked out by whoever ran first.

04 Heroes

Every run feeds a hero.

Coins from your runs unlock pixel-art heroes and evolve them through 15 forms. A run under 5 km pays 1 coin and every further 5 km adds another; a daily login bonus grows across a 7-day streak. Everyone starts with one hero, picked at random.

05 Underneath it all

Still a proper run tracker.

  1. 01

    Live tracking

    Distance, time, pace and hexes crossed, refreshed every 1.5 seconds.

  2. 02

    Screen off, still running

    Background tracking on iOS and Android, with pause and stop on the lock screen.

  3. 03

    Works with no signal

    Finish a run offline and it's queued, then synced when you're back online.

  4. 04

    Feed, friends, chat

    Post a run with its route map. Follow friends, like, comment, message, and talk tactics in clan chat.

  5. 05

    Replay cards

    Every claimed loop gets a short animated replay you can share as an image.
